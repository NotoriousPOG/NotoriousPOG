### About Me

- I build software that runs on **your own computer and network** — shop tools, label printers, camera review apps, and Arma Reforger mods
- Lately shipping **practical AI governance** tooling on public standards ([NIST AI RMF](https://www.nist.gov/itl/ai-risk-management-framework))
- Comfortable across **TypeScript, Python, and C**, from desktop UX to LAN services
- Happy to chat about local-first tools, thermal printing, game-server mods, or responsible AI assessments
- Open source lives here: [@NotoriousPOG](https://github.com/NotoriousPOG)
